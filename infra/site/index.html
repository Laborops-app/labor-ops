Everything a crew company runs on

From the first quote to the last paycheck.

Built with stage hands, riggers and labor coordinators, for the way live events actually run: early calls, long days, last-minute changes.

Schedule a whole show in minutes

Events split into day phases, shifts by role, and a crew picker that hides who is booked or unavailable and puts your best-rated people first.

Clock in from any phone, even with no signal

Crew install it from the browser (no app store). Punches are saved offline with the real time and GPS, and sync when signal comes back.

Timesheets that approve themselves

Time inside the scheduled hours is approved automatically. Minimum calls, overtime, double time and day rates are priced for you.

Payroll and invoices, done

Export to ADP (RUN and Workforce Now), Gusto or QuickBooks, and invoice the client from approved hours in one click.

Know your margin on every event

Labor cost against what you bill, fill rate, overtime, no-shows and labor budget, per event and per pay period.

Leads and on-site coordinators

Give a crew lead their team for the day: check-ins, time fixes and ratings, without seeing anything else.

Alerts that actually reach crew

Push first, then a text only if it was not opened. Late and no-show alerts go to the right coordinator and lead.

Pipeline from quote to archive

Quotes your customers accept online, a board from pending to completed, a “ready to invoice” nudge for HR, and an archive to book again next year.

Your whole crew in one place

Skills, certificates with expiry, availability, ratings, groups, chat and training, with each company’s data fully separated.

How it works

Up and running in an afternoon.

  1. Add your roles and crewPay and bill rates per role. Crew get an invite and add LaborOps to their phone's home screen.
  2. Build the eventPhases for each day, shifts by role, and offers that crew accept with a tap. Leads get their team.
  3. Pay and invoiceHours approve themselves, overtime is priced, and payroll and the client invoice are one export each.
